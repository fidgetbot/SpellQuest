## Shared prompt specification

Square production fantasy adventure map, richly detailed ink-edged watercolor/gouache storybook illustration, high overhead bird's-eye projection at uniform scale. No horizon or foreground framing; no text, letters, labels, numbers, markers, icons, UI, borders, dotted routes or arrows. Real geography with connected branching navigable roads/corridors appropriate to the setting. Routes connect doors and landmarks, never cut through walls, trees or open water; water crossings use visible bridges or walkways. Readable at phone size. No characters. Preserve distinct landmark areas for live HTML pins.
