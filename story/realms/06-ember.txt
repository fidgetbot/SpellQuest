# The ash road
You climb toward Ember Peaks through a snowfall of warm ash.
Pip rides inside a damp scarf to protect his nose.
Tavi wraps her tools before the fine dust can clog them.
A shepherd flags you down beside a collapsed hillside path.
You help him lower two frightened goats onto safer ground.
He points toward a village whose roof bells ring in warning.
You reach its square as glowing stones strike the outer fields.
The villagers have gathered their youngest and oldest near the well.
You unfold the scorched map beside the village captain.
The locked tunnel is their only route away from the mountain.
You offer to open it while the captain organizes the evacuation.
Tavi checks the sky and says you cannot waste a bell.

# The sealed tunnel
You reach the tunnel beneath a gate of heat-warped iron.
The Regent's lock glows with a cold light against the hot stone.
You shield Tavi while she examines its buried hinges.
Pip scratches at a narrow ventilation shaft beside the entrance.
You hear a faint answer from somewhere beyond the gate.
Workers are trapped inside with the supplies meant for the village.
You slide water through the shaft in a covered tin.
Tavi finds a gear forced backward to hold the gate shut.
You use the blade as a lever rather than a weapon.
The gear turns, and the trapped workers pull from inside.
Together, you lift the gate high enough for people to crawl beneath.
You leave a timber brace where the Regent left a lock.

# The long line
You return to the village and lead the first families toward the tunnel.
Tavi marks safe turns with bright strips from her work apron.
Pip runs beside a child who refuses to leave her frightened pony.
You take the pony's rope and let the child set the pace.
A tremor scatters loose stones across the line of travelers.
You hold your shield over an elderly potter until the shaking stops.
The potter saves one small bowl and abandons the rest.
You carry it while he helps another traveler stand.
At the tunnel, the workers welcome everyone with lamps and water.
You count each family against the captain's list.
Three names remain beside a farm above the eastern ridge.
You hand the bowl to Tavi and climb back into the ash.

# Above the ridge
You find the farm's door buried beneath fallen roof tiles.
Two siblings answer when you call through the kitchen window.
Their grandfather is trapped beneath a beam inside.
You crawl through and wedge the shield under the timber.
Pip stays beside the children while you lift.
The grandfather pulls himself clear as the silver border bends.
You guide them through the window one at a time.
A stream of hot mud crosses the path below the farm.
You unroll a fence along its narrowest point.
The family crosses while you steady the trembling posts.
Behind them, the farmhouse settles slowly into the empty hillside.
You carry the smallest child toward the tunnel's waiting lights.

# The caged flame
You hear a thin song beneath the mountain's deeper rumble.
Its notes come from a stone shrine beside the evacuation road.
You find a firebird chained above a sealed volcanic vent.
Each frightened wingbeat drives more heat into the mountain.
Tavi returns from the tunnel and reads the shrine's machinery.
The Regent has turned the captive bird into a pressure engine.
You lower your blade so the creature will not mistake you for a guard.
Pip lies on his back, offering the least threatening greeting he knows.
The bird's frantic song softens into three trembling notes.
You promise to open the cage without asking anything in return.
Tavi finds a release that must be turned from beneath the vent.
You pull your scarf tight and descend the narrow service ladder.

# The vent opens
You step onto a platform shaking above a red-lit chasm.
The release wheel is chained to a spike of hardened ink.
You chip away the spike while hot air batters your shield.
Above, Tavi calls the timing between the mountain's pressure surges.
You wait through one roar and turn the wheel during the silence.
A vent opens toward an uninhabited valley beyond the ridge.
The trapped heat escapes in a towering plume of golden steam.
You climb as the platform begins folding into the wall.
Tavi catches your wrist at the final rung.
The cage opens, and the firebird rises without looking back.
You watch it disappear into the clean air above the ash.
The mountain's terrible shaking settles into an ordinary distant growl.

# A refuge takes shape
You reach the far end of the tunnel beneath a pale evening sky.
Forest escorts wait there with the supplies promised at Rootbound Court.
You help pitch shelters beside a stream clear enough for cooking.
The potter sets his saved bowl in the center of a shared table.
Tavi uses the empty supply crates to build sturdy sleeping platforms.
You return the children to their grandfather beside a warm fire.
Pip accepts an enthusiastic thank-you from the pony.
The village captain crosses the last three names from her list.
You stand quietly as the refugees name their new settlement Dawnrest.
A familiar three-note song drifts down from the darkening sky.
The freed firebird lands beside your bent shield and warms its rim.
You welcome the companion who came back by choice.

# Wings for the road
The firebird folds its wings when Tavi calls it Cinder.
You polish the shield's straightened edge in the morning light.
Dawnrest's first watchtower rises from rescued tunnel timbers.
The captain promises a safe welcome for travelers following behind.
You hear that the sky bridges above the mountains have broken.
Cinder circles toward them, leaving a brief trail of golden sparks.
You secure the memory lamp beside Tavi's toolkit.
Pip studies the clouds with understandable suspicion.
Together, you climb toward a road that hangs above the world.
