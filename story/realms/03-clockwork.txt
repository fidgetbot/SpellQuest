# The closed city
You reach Clockwork City beneath a rain of harmless metal filings.
Every gate is sealed, though the streets behind them are crowded.
A girl lowers a speaking tube from the wall.
She introduces herself as Tavi and asks whether you can climb.
You show her the shipping order through the bars.
Her expression changes when she sees the Regent's seal.
You catch the rope she throws from a maintenance balcony.
Pip rides inside your pack as you scale the wall.
Below, a siege engine rolls toward the silent gate.
Tavi pulls you onto the balcony before its searchlight arrives.
You follow her past clocks whose hands all point to midnight.
The city has been forced to make the same moment forever.

# The stalled heartbeat
You enter a workshop filled with unfinished mechanical birds.
Tavi's father lies asleep beneath a blanket beside the furnace.
She explains that workers lose a memory with every commanded shift.
You raise the lamp and see stolen days trapped in the gears.
One small gear holds the memory of a family picnic.
You ease it from the assembly without breaking its teeth.
Tavi's father opens his eyes and remembers her birthday.
You stop the remaining conveyor before it can take another shift.
A warning bell rings inside the ceiling instead of outside.
Tavi grabs her tools and points toward the central regulator.
You carry the recovered gear while she gathers a repair kit.
Together, you set out to stop the city's stolen heartbeat.

# The walking barricade
You cross a street blocked by a six-legged iron barricade.
It demands work papers from everyone trying to pass.
Tavi offers an old maintenance badge while you study its joints.
The badge is rejected, and the barricade unfolds two grabbing arms.
You catch one arm against the shield and slide beneath it.
Pip darts through the gap carrying Tavi's smallest wrench.
You loosen a panel while she distracts the machine.
Inside, a frightened guard's memory circles a black spindle.
You lift the spindle free instead of smashing the mechanism.
The barricade remembers its duty to protect the people.
It folds its arms into a bridge across the street.
You lead the waiting families beneath its newly raised legs.

# Under the foundry
You enter the foundry through a warm drainage tunnel.
The copied order matches numbers carved on crates overhead.
You open one and discover hundreds of bottled working days.
Tavi finds her own first invention shining inside a vial.
You return it, and she suddenly remembers a hidden passage.
The passage runs beneath the regulator's enormous flywheel.
You crawl through while its spokes sweep inches above your back.
At the far end, an overseer lowers a cage around Tavi.
You wedge the shield beneath its descending edge.
Pip carries the latch key out through a narrow opening.
You free Tavi before the overseer can call another machine.
She pockets the key and refuses to leave anyone else behind.

# The wrong repair
You reach the regulator and find its brake lever glowing red.
Tavi warns that pulling it would stop the city's water pumps.
You examine the lamp's memory of the old floodgate wheels.
Their counterweight design gives her a different idea.
You help her build a bypass from spare conveyor links.
Outside, the siege engine begins hammering the western wall.
Dust rains over the open regulator as you hold its casing.
Tavi threads the new chain around a spinning axle.
You guide the chain with your blade's unsharpened back.
The bypass catches, and the water pumps continue turning freely.
Now you pull the brake without silencing the city.
The command spindle slows enough for Tavi to remove it.

# The gate holds
You carry the black spindle toward the siege engine's searchlight.
Tavi knows its signal will draw the engine away from the gate.
You run along the wall while its iron head follows.
Pip crouches beside a bell rope at the corner tower.
You throw the spindle into an empty loading yard.
The engine turns and rolls beneath the raised freight crane.
Pip pulls the rope as you release the crane's counterweight.
A cargo net drops across the engine's steering arms.
Tavi climbs onto its back and disconnects the command receiver.
Its wheels stop just short of the workers' shelter.
You open the yard doors so the driver can climb out.
The siege ends with a rescued person rather than a wreck.

# A city remembers
You return to the foundry and open the memory crates.
Tavi's father organizes workers to carry them through the streets.
You deliver a baker's lost recipe to a waiting oven.
Pip follows the smell until somebody offers him a crust.
Clocks begin marking different hours as their owners wind them.
You help turn the walking barricade into a public footbridge.
Tavi repairs the siege engine for hauling stone and timber.
Its former driver volunteers to mend the damaged western wall.
You see the city choosing uses for its own machines.
A merchant named Venn arrives with news from the desert.
He claims to know a route the Regent cannot watch.
You agree to meet him beyond the eastern gate.

# An engineer's decision
Tavi packs her tools before you can ask for help.
She says repairing one city means little while others remain trapped.
You make room beside the lamp for her folding toolkit.
Her father gives her a compass case without a compass.
He expects her to fill it with something worth finding.
You leave the city beneath clocks ringing their true hours.
Behind you, the gate remains open for the first time in months.
Venn waits beside three camels and a mirror-bright road.
You follow him east with Pip and Tavi at your side.
