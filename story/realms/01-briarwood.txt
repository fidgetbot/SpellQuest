# The broken gate
You arrive at Briarwood carrying a blade that has forgotten its light.
Beyond the broken gate, a fox hangs tangled in black ribbon.
You cut the lowest strand before it tightens around his paw.
The ribbon recoils and slaps a warning against the stones.
You wedge your empty scabbard beneath the falling gate.
The fox wriggles free and lands inside your open cloak.
A brass tag beneath his fur bears the name Pip.
You offer him water while distant bells ring without sound.
Pip nudges your blade toward a lantern buried in ivy.
You lift the lantern and find a tiny flame inside.
Its warmth wakes a narrow silver line along your blade.
Together, you step beneath the gate before the ribbons return.

# A road that bites
You follow cart tracks ending abruptly at a wall of thorns.
A wagon bell trembles behind the branches, though nobody rings it.
You push your shieldless arm inside and catch a falling basket.
Pip pulls your sleeve away from a snapping thorn.
You use the basket to hold the nearest branches apart.
An old courier crawls through the opening with a torn satchel.
She points toward a wheel trapped beneath a black root.
You brace the wagon while she climbs out of danger.
The root lashes at Pip, and your blade catches its shadow.
Silver light peels the darkness away without cutting the wood.
The freed root settles gently into the soil.
The courier says the forest is being forced to fight.

# The hollow sentinel
You escort the courier to a shelter inside an enormous stump.
A stone guardian blocks the entrance with a raised hammer.
You notice moss trembling beneath its black iron collar.
Pip circles behind it while you draw the hammer aside.
The blow cracks a boulder where your boots stood.
You scramble over the fragments and reach the collar.
A strip of living ink seals its clasp.
You press your blade beneath the strip and lift.
The collar falls, and the hammer stops above your head.
The guardian kneels to clear the shelter's entrance.
Inside, frightened travelers offer you a wooden shield.
You understand that freeing a protector can win a battle.

# The hidden workshop
You examine the shield and discover an unfinished silver border.
The courier remembers a smith who worked beneath the mill.
You follow the dry stream while Pip scouts the bank.
At the mill, turning gears grind against an invisible chain.
You jam a fallen branch between two empty teeth.
Pip slips through the stopped wheel and finds a latch.
You enter a workshop lit by jars of captured daylight.
A smith named Orra emerges from behind a workbench.
She recognizes your blade as a keeper's unfinished promise.
You hold the shield steady while she repairs its border.
Your blade's light flows into the silver and stays.
Orra asks you to carry protection where fear has spread.

# The first defense
You leave the workshop as black ribbons pour over the mill.
The courtyard fills with wooden masks riding thorny bodies.
You plant the new shield between them and the doorway.
Their first rush scatters sparks across the silver edge.
Pip drags a loose rope beneath the nearest attacker.
You pull the rope and send its mask spinning.
Without the mask, its branches become harmless young saplings.
You turn your shield to reflect light onto the others.
The masks crack one after another in the reflected dawn.
Orra opens the mill doors to let the trapped workers escape.
You hold the courtyard until the last child crosses.
Behind you, the wheel begins turning with ordinary water.

# The stolen signpost
You lead the workers toward the village along an unfamiliar fork.
Every signpost has been scraped blank by the Regent's servants.
Pip sniffs the courier's satchel and follows its familiar leather.
You discover a ditch filled with stolen wooden signs.
A ribbon creature rises when you reach for one.
You pin its shadow beneath the shield's bright rim.
The courier retrieves the village sign while you hold firm.
Its carved name returns to the road like a remembered song.
You raise the sign and the fog parts around familiar roofs.
The workers run ahead to embrace their waiting families.
You set the remaining signs upright along the crossroads.
For the first time, travelers can choose their own direction.

# Beneath the bell
You climb the village bell tower with Pip at your heels.
Inside the bell hangs a dark knot swallowing every sound.
You strike the knot, but your blade passes through it.
Pip drops the brass tag from his collar onto the floor.
Its small clear chime leaves a crack in the darkness.
You tap your shield in answer and the crack widens.
Below, the villagers begin beating pots and wooden spoons.
You add your blade's ringing note to their rising rhythm.
The knot bursts into a flock of harmless paper scraps.
The bell's first true peal rolls across the forest.
Far away, bound guardians lift their heads toward the sound.
You see the road to the river shining between them.

# A promise carried onward
You return the courier's satchel with every rescued letter inside.
She gives you a sealed message addressed to the Drowned Archives.
Orra fastens a stronger strap onto your light-forged shield.
The freed guardian takes its place beside the repaired gate.
Pip settles beside your pack instead of returning to the woods.
You ask whether he intends to come, and his tail answers.
The village lights remain bright as you reach the river road.
Across the water, library towers stand half beneath the waves.
You carry Briarwood's message toward the bells beneath the lake.
