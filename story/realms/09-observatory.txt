# Stars below your feet
You moor Dawnrunner beside an arch suspended over a still lake.
Beneath the arch, the observatory hangs with its windows facing downward.
Tavi secures two safety ropes before you leave the deck.
You follow a spiral stair into rooms that challenge your balance.
Pip keeps one paw against your boot at every turn.
Cinder's reflection flies below him in the polished floor.
An astronomer named Ilyan waits beside an enormous star lens.
He welcomes the memory lamp before asking your name.
You show him the station map recovered from the coast.
His face tightens when he recognizes the Regent's listening pattern.
You ask whether the stars can reveal its weakness.
Ilyan says they can reveal which memories the Regent fears.

# A sky that disagrees
You compare three star charts spread across a curved table.
Each chart gives a different date for the same vanished constellation.
Ilyan insists all three were copied faithfully.
You raise the lamp above their overlapping lines.
A fourth pattern appears where their differences meet.
Tavi traces it onto a transparent sheet.
You recognize the outline of the Crown from the Archives record.
The Regent removed its history from different books in different ways.
The contradictions have preserved what his corrections tried to hide.
You fit the sheet over the station map.
A path emerges toward the kingdom's oldest library.
Before following it, you need to understand what the Crown actually does.

# The archivist's loss
You place the bronze record's copied image beneath the star lens.
Ilyan turns a wheel until reflected moonlight touches its edge.
A room appears inside the glass, filled with falling ash.
You see the young archivist carrying books through a burning doorway.
He searches every face outside for someone who never appears.
A small nameplate remains in his hand after the memory fades.
You read the name of his teacher, keeper of the first library.
Ilyan explains that the fire took records as well as lives.
The archivist began saving memories because he feared another disappearance.
You remember how locked doors replaced readers in the bronze record.
Grief explains his fear without excusing what he has done.
You decide the ending must restore people, not erase another person.

# The crown's promise
You find a cabinet labeled with the vanished constellation's shape.
Its door opens when the three conflicting charts are placed together.
Inside lies a model Crown with removable jewel sockets.
Ilyan shows you how each jewel holds a freely remembered word.
The Crown cannot restore a history built from forced repetition.
You test one earned jewel inside the model's smallest socket.
A forgotten gardener's name appears on the observatory window.
Tavi recognizes the name from a memorial in Clockwork City.
You understand that your learning can return more than stolen sounds.
Ilyan warns that an incomplete Crown cannot safely face the Regent.
You promise to finish the work rather than rush its power.
The model guides your next task without replacing the real Crown.

# The false constellation
You step onto the viewing platform as its stars begin moving.
A new constellation spells a command across the reflected lake.
The Regent has noticed someone reading the memories he buried.
You lower your eyes before the command can take hold.
Pip pulls Ilyan away from the lens with both front paws.
Tavi jams the turning wheel with her strongest wrench.
The platform tilts as the command pushes against its supports.
You raise the shield and catch the false constellation in its reflection.
Cinder burns a bright line across the reflected pattern.
The command breaks into separate harmless points of light.
You help Ilyan close the lens before another signal arrives.
The stars return to their patient positions beyond the glass.

# What the teacher left
You search the lens housing for damage from the attack.
A hidden note slides out beneath a brass retaining ring.
It was written by the teacher the Regent believed entirely lost.
You hold it beneath ordinary lamplight instead of a memory spell.
The note says a life is larger than any perfect record.
Its final instruction asks future keepers to leave room for change.
You read it aloud while Ilyan keeps the lens shuttered.
Tavi says the Regent has mistaken holding on for keeping safe.
You place the note inside the promise compass's case.
It will need to reach him when he can finally listen.
Pip rests his chin on the case as if guarding an egg.
You add the teacher's unfinished hope to the things you carry.

# The advancing storm
You open the observatory's outer shutters at dawn.
Below, dark banners move across the Ashen March toward the restored roads.
The Regent is answering your discoveries with a counterattack.
You signal the coast before the clouds swallow the horizon.
Neri's lamps pass the warning from harbor to harbor.
Ilyan sends copies of the true star route with every messenger.
Tavi prepares Dawnrunner for carrying people instead of cargo.
You ask the astronomer to shelter anyone fleeing the storm.
He opens the observatory's unused rooms without waiting for another request.
Cinder rises above the arch to scout a safe flight path.
You see campfires gathering where the forest roads meet the plain.
Your earlier victories are becoming people who can stand beside you.

# A different kind of strength
You leave the model Crown with Ilyan for future keepers to study.
He gives you the route to the Last Library on weatherproof cloth.
You secure the teacher's note beside the recovered memories.
Tavi checks that Dawnrunner has enough rope for rescue flights.
Pip claims a lookout spot where he can see both companions.
Cinder returns with ash on his feathers and urgency in his song.
You steer toward the gathering camps rather than the distant fortress.
The kingdom needs protection before it needs a final duel.
You descend into the March carrying a plan built from everything you learned.
