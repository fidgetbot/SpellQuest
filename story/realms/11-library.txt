# The door that remembers
You find the Last Library inside a cliff shaped like a closed book.
Its entrance has no handle, only an outline of an open hand.
You place your palm there and offer your name freely.
The stone warms without trying to take anything from you.
Pip steps through first when the doorway unfolds.
Inside, shelves climb higher than Dawnrunner's tallest mast.
Tavi spots fresh ink moving across labels that should be still.
The Regent is rewriting the records from somewhere beyond the walls.
You lift the memory lamp to preserve the nearest changing title.
Cinder lands on a lamp stand and fills the aisle with warmth.
You decide to protect the originals before searching for the Crown.
The first shelf steadies beneath a name you refuse to let vanish.

# A librarian of paper
You meet a figure assembled from folded catalog pages.
It introduces itself as Index and bows without bending its books.
You show the route Ilyan sent from the observatory.
Index recognizes the handwriting of its oldest surviving friend.
It explains that the Crown rests where freely shared histories meet.
The Regent has been sealing those histories into private cabinets.
You ask how many cabinets must be opened.
Index turns toward a hall extending beyond the visible lamps.
Tavi studies its lock system instead of counting the doors.
You recognize each lock as part of a larger command circuit.
One central ledger can release them without breaking their records.
Index leads you toward the desk that once belonged to the Regent.

# The changing corridor
You enter a hallway that lengthens whenever you remember its end.
The Regent has written an instruction forcing it to continue forever.
You stop walking and ask Tavi to mark where you stand.
Pip follows your scent backward instead of looking at the walls.
You tie a rope between his harness and your shield.
Cinder lights the rope so every twist remains visible.
Together, you follow the path you actually traveled.
The false distance peels away like loose wallpaper.
You reach a small wooden door hidden beneath the grand illusion.
Index laughs with the sound of turning pages.
You have escaped by trusting evidence rather than the corridor's claim.
The Regent's old desk waits on the other side.

# The keeper's ledger
You open the central ledger and find pages locked by fear.
One says that a forgotten name is a life wasted.
Another insists that change is simply a slower kind of loss.
You place the teacher's note beside those rigid commands.
Its ordinary handwriting refuses to transform into an order.
Index reads the sentence about leaving room for change.
A lock opens somewhere in the cabinet hall.
You share memories of Briarwood growing after its rescue.
Tavi adds the city choosing new uses for its machines.
The ledger's iron corners soften as different truths stand together.
You turn the last clasp without tearing a single page.
Across the library, cabinet doors swing open for everyone.

# The originals return
You carry the first released history to its proper shelf.
Its pages remember a village before anyone needed to rescue it.
Tavi finds designs for inventions her city has not built yet.
Index explains that the future was never the Regent's to preserve.
You stop a stream of ink trying to cross out an unfinished sketch.
The shield's light holds it back without touching the paper.
Pip brings loose pages from beneath the cabinets one careful mouthful at a time.
Cinder dries the spilled ink so it cannot spread farther.
You ask Index to make copies for the restored Archives.
Knowledge will be safer with many keepers than one locked room.
The library begins preparing parcels for roads you helped reopen.
You watch a defense become a promise to share.

# The Crown assembled
You enter the central reading circle carrying your earned jewels.
An empty circlet rises from a table surrounded by ordinary chairs.
There is no throne inside the room that keeps it.
Tavi checks the sockets against the model remembered from the observatory.
You fit the first jewel and hear a distant voice return.
Each new setting holds a different fragment of restored history.
Index warns that the remaining spaces must be filled through real learning.
You cannot force the final spell or borrow another keeper's victories.
The circlet accepts its unfinished shape without shattering.
You wrap it carefully for the journey still ahead.
Pip sits beside the bundle as if appointed its official guardian.
The Crown is assembled, but its greatest work is not yet ready.

# The Regent's interruption
You hear the library's walls whisper a command to close.
Ink pours beneath the distant entrance like an incoming tide.
The Regent is trying to seal every witness inside.
You give Index the shipping route to the reopened Archives.
It sends the first parcels through a narrow service passage.
Tavi braces the main door with a shelf emptied for evacuation.
You stand behind the shield while Cinder burns a safe line across the floor.
The tide divides around that line instead of reaching the books.
Pip guides Index toward the service passage with urgent little barks.
You retreat only after the last catalog page leaves the room.
Tavi releases the brace from the safe side of the threshold.
The door closes on empty aisles whose histories are already traveling outward.

# The witness carried
You emerge above the valley with the Crown and the originals safe.
Index chooses to accompany the parcels to Sella's welcoming Archives.
You keep a copy of the ledger's released first page.
It proves the Regent's commands can be answered without erasing their author.
Tavi loads the unfinished Crown aboard Dawnrunner.
Cinder scouts the black towers beyond the valley.
Pip refuses to leave the bundle unattended even for supper.
You steer toward the Unwritten Citadel with the teacher's note close at hand.
This time, the Regent will face people whose histories he could not keep apart.
