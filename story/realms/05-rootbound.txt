# The divided bridge
You reach Rootbound Court as arrows of bark strike an empty bridge.
Neither bank allows travelers to cross the river.
On one side, oak-cloaked wardens accuse the willows of stealing rain.
Across the water, willow riders blame the oaks for poisoned roots.
You raise your shield and stand where both sides can see you.
Pip squeezes beneath the barricade to comfort a trapped deer.
You ask for enough silence to bring the animal out.
Tavi lowers a plank while the archers reluctantly wait.
You guide the deer across before its shaking legs give way.
Both sides cheer, then look embarrassed by their agreement.
You ask them to spare the bridge until morning.
They grant you one night to find what poisoned their water.

# Upstream evidence
You follow the river upstream with a warden from each bank.
The oak scout carries a spear carved from fallen wood.
The willow rider carries medicine instead of a weapon.
You ask them to test the same pool rather than trade accusations.
Both bowls turn black beneath the moonlight.
Pip discovers dark threads caught on a submerged branch.
You lift the branch and find a strip of military canvas.
Tavi recognizes the fastening used on the Regent's supply wagons.
The two scouts finally kneel beside the same clue.
You follow wheel tracks toward an abandoned charcoal camp.
Fresh smoke rises where its chimney should be cold.
Someone has been feeding the feud from outside the valley.

# The false witnesses
You watch masked workers pour ink into the river's headwater.
Their uniforms alternate between oak green and willow silver.
You understand why each side saw the other's soldiers.
Tavi sketches the wagon's crest while you approach the sluice.
Pip pulls a loose canvas flap across the workers' lantern.
In the sudden darkness, you close the poison valve.
One worker swings a hook toward the willow rider.
The oak scout blocks it before you can raise your shield.
You disarm the worker and keep him away from the water.
He admits that the Regent promised payment for every ruined tree.
You gather the false uniforms and seal the remaining barrels.
The scouts carry the evidence together on the return journey.

# The court beneath roots
You enter a council chamber woven beneath an ancient living tree.
The two leaders sit with a river stone between their chairs.
You lay the uniforms beside the poisoned water samples.
The oak leader recognizes a cloak missing from a raided caravan.
The willow leader remembers medicine stolen during the same attack.
You explain how both losses became weapons against them.
Neither leader wants to be first to admit the mistake.
Pip climbs onto the river stone and falls asleep.
Tavi asks whether a fox should finish their meeting for them.
A ripple of laughter loosens the chamber's angry silence.
You propose repairing the water before deciding who deserves an apology.
Both leaders stand when you offer the first shovel.

# The river's wound
You return upstream with workers from both forest clans.
Oak builders brace the damaged banks with fallen trunks.
Willow healers weave filters from living reeds.
You lift poisoned stones into Tavi's sealed metal baskets.
The memory lamp reveals where the river flowed before the diversion.
You mark the old channel with strips of clean cloth.
A buried gate resists beneath a weight of dark sludge.
You pull while workers on both banks hold the ropes.
The gate rises and fresh water pours into the waiting channel.
Pip dances backward from the first bright splash.
You watch the black pool shrink behind the reed filters.
By sunset, a silver fish crosses water nobody claims to own.

# The Regent's answer
You hear wagon wheels returning before the workers finish.
The Regent's hired raiders see the repaired river and draw back.
Their captain orders the two clans to resume their quarrel.
You step between him and the joined work crews.
The oak scout raises a shield beside yours.
The willow rider passes medicine to the workers behind you.
Tavi tips an empty wagon across the raiders' approach.
You catch their hooked ladder and lower it harmlessly into mud.
Their false banners are torn away by the evening wind.
Without the disguise, the captain cannot divide the people facing him.
You offer the raiders a safe retreat without their poison barrels.
They leave the valley with far less than they brought.

# An alliance with work
You help lift the barricades from the river bridge.
The leaders meet halfway with no stone between them.
You ask for watchposts that protect travelers from either direction.
The oak builders begin measuring before the speeches are finished.
The willow healers open a shared shelter beside the crossing.
Tavi teaches their apprentices to repair the sealed filter baskets.
You show the scouts how to recognize command collars on guardians.
Freed protectors from Briarwood arrive along the reopened road.
The valley greets them without reaching for spears.
You see an alliance becoming useful before it becomes ceremonial.
Both leaders promise escorts for refugees from the burning mountains.
You look east and notice red light beneath the clouds.

# The ember warning
A messenger stumbles onto the bridge carrying a scorched map.
You steady her while the willow healers bring water.
She says Ember Peaks has begun throwing fire across its villages.
The Regent's soldiers have locked the safest mountain tunnel.
You promise to open it before the next eruption.
The leaders send supplies along the roads you helped restore.
Pip chooses the messenger's trail without waiting for the compass.
You follow him as ash settles softly onto the river.
Behind you, the bridge stays open beneath both banners.
