# A harbor without calls
You land in a harbor where gulls cry above speechless people.
The keeper introduces herself by writing Neri on a slate.
She points toward a black buoy beyond the breakwater.
Every attempt to speak sends a dark ripple toward it.
You ask questions with drawings while Tavi unloads supplies.
Pip quickly learns which hand signal means fresh water.
Neri draws a bell surrounded by smaller bells along the coast.
You recognize a network rather than a single cursed object.
Cinder tries one note and recoils from its returning echo.
You move the firebird behind your shield until the echo fades.
Neri leads you to a cellar lined with thick woven mats.
Inside, you can plan without feeding the listening buoy.

# Hands that understand
You spend the morning learning the harbor's useful signs.
Neri shows you warnings for falling ropes and shifting tides.
You practice until your hands can answer without hesitation.
Tavi sketches the buoy's shape beside the clockwork regulator.
Their command receivers share the same narrow spindle.
You propose disconnecting it during the lowest tide.
Neri gathers divers with ropes instead of sending them alone.
Pip helps carry light signal flags between the waiting crews.
You test the signs from opposite ends of the quay.
A mistake sends the wrong flag up, and everyone patiently resets.
By noon, you can coordinate a rescue without one shouted order.
You take that skill aboard a boat smaller than Dawnrunner.

# Beneath the buoy
You row toward the black buoy beneath a bank of fog.
Neri signals the changing current with two fingers.
You anchor outside the reach of its trailing chains.
Tavi lowers a sealed inspection lamp over the side.
The buoy's shadow contains a staircase built entirely from stolen sound.
You use the memory lamp to hold its steps steady.
A diver ties your safety line around the lowest chain.
You descend until the quay becomes a trembling silver line overhead.
Inside the buoy, empty mouths are carved around a spindle.
You cover them with cloth before they can drink your breath.
Tavi's tool fits the inspection bolt exactly.
You open the casing and discover three cables running inland.

# The wrong target
You resist the urge to cut the spindle loose immediately.
The cables carry trapped voices as well as the Regent's commands.
Neri's slate warns that breaking them could scatter the voices forever.
You mark each cable with a different ribbon.
The first leads to a net maker's ruined warehouse.
The second disappears beneath the old lighthouse.
The third trembles whenever Cinder moves on the waiting boat.
You realize the device is searching for the firebird's song.
You signal Tavi to move Cinder safely behind the breakwater.
The third cable slackens without another voice to chase.
You close the casing carefully and return to the surface.
Now you know what must be protected before the machine stops.

# The warehouse choir
You enter the warehouse through a door tied shut with fishing line.
Shelves of glass shells fill every wall from floor to rafters.
Neri recognizes her father's laugh moving inside one shell.
You trace their shared cable to a locked collection box.
The memory lamp reveals marks left by the last repair crew.
Tavi follows them to a concealed release behind the shelves.
You hold the box steady while she opens its catches.
The shells tremble, but their voices remain safely inside.
Neri helps carry them into padded fishing baskets.
You refuse to leave the smallest shell beneath a broken plank.
Pip noses it free and places it beside the others.
Together, you bring the harbor's stolen choir back to the quay.

# The lighthouse signal
You climb the lighthouse while Neri organizes the waiting families.
At the top, a spinning lens sends darkness instead of light.
You block its beam with your silver-bordered shield.
Tavi removes the command spindle from the lens mechanism.
The beam stops, and the harbor's dark ripples fall still.
You signal the boat crew to disconnect the marked cables.
Below, three flags rise in the order you practiced.
You turn the lighthouse toward the baskets of recovered shells.
Cinder sings one clear note from the safe side of the shield.
The shells open gently beneath the restored beam.
Voices return to their owners like birds finding familiar windows.
Neri calls her father's name before she remembers to breathe.

# The coast answers
You stand beside the keeper while the harbor speaks again.
Some voices laugh, and others begin with a long quiet thank-you.
You help Tavi dismantle the receiver rather than merely switch it off.
Its map reveals more listening stations leading toward an observatory.
The Regent's spell travels through echoes of remembered commands.
You copy the station map before Neri locks the device away.
The harbor crews volunteer their lamps for a coastwide warning.
You teach them the storm signals that brought your party together.
By nightfall, safe lights answer along the entire shore.
Cinder sings from the lighthouse without any dark echo returning.
You realize the kingdom is learning to answer the Regent together.
Neri offers Dawnrunner a repaired sail for the journey inland.

# The star road
You spread the station map across Dawnrunner's deck.
Tavi aligns it with the sky chart from the floating islands.
Every line converges on a tower hanging beneath a stone arch.
The tower's observatory was built upside down to watch reflected stars.
Pip studies the drawing and retreats from the railing.
You promise that the ship will land before anyone walks there.
Neri waves both hands in the sign for a safe return.
You answer in the same language as Dawnrunner rises.
The restored harbor remains bright behind your climbing ship.
