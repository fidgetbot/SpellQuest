# The missing span
You reach a staircase ending in open blue air.
Beyond it, the Skychain Isles drift on enormous silver anchors.
A broken bridge swings beneath the nearest floating orchard.
Tavi counts the remaining links while you test the first support.
Pip refuses the edge until you fasten him safely inside your pack.
Cinder flies across carrying the lightest end of your rope.
You secure it around the stair's stone crown.
Tavi crosses first with a harness made from shield straps.
You follow as the clouds open beneath your boots.
Halfway across, a sudden gust pulls the orchard farther away.
You hold the rope while Tavi locks its end around a tree.
Together, you reach the grass just before the staircase vanishes into mist.

# The storm between
You find a deserted waystation beneath the orchard's silver branches.
A sky chart shows three islands drifting out of their proper positions.
Tavi believes the central anchor engine has lost its governor.
You leave Pip beside her while you inspect the observation deck.
Cinder gives a sharp cry as lightning strikes an empty tower.
The island tilts, and a gap opens across the waystation floor.
You jump backward as the central rooms break free.
Tavi and Pip remain safe inside the drifting half.
You throw the compass case across before the distance grows.
She catches it and points toward the chart's central island.
You promise to meet there rather than chase blindly into the storm.
Cinder settles beside you as the clouds swallow your companions.

# A separate crossing
You search the remaining rooms for anything that can carry you.
An old glider hangs above a rack of weather instruments.
You patch its torn fabric with the cloak that sheltered Pip.
Cinder warms the damp seams until they hold.
You read the wind vanes instead of trusting the shifting horizon.
The first gust lifts the glider without lifting you.
You tighten the harness and wait for the second.
Then you leap from the deck into a rising current.
Cinder guides you around the storm's darkest edge.
You steer toward a small island shaped like a sleeping whale.
A landing net catches the glider between two abandoned posts.
You climb out trembling, but closer to the central anchor.

# The signal lesson
You discover a signal lamp inside the island's empty watchhouse.
Its shutter works, though its wick has burned away.
Cinder offers a small flame that does not consume the brass.
You flash the three-note rhythm that first calmed the firebird.
A distant light answers from behind a curtain of rain.
Tavi has recognized the rhythm and found another lamp.
You exchange simple signals for safe ground and dangerous wind.
Her light moves steadily toward the central island.
You mark your own route using the old watchhouse chart.
For the first time since the storm, you know everyone is alive.
You pack the signal lamp beside the memory lamp.
Cinder waits at the door while the rain begins to clear.

# The anchor engine
You reach the central island by a chain broad enough to walk.
Its engine room rocks with each uneven turn of the governor.
You find a black wedge jamming the largest balance arm.
Pulling it now would let the entire mechanism fall sideways.
You brace the arm with your shield and search for a counterweight.
A familiar wrench lands beside your boot.
Tavi climbs down from a service hatch with Pip behind her.
You laugh with relief while she secures a temporary support.
Pip presses his forehead against your knee before inspecting the engine.
Together, you release the wedge one careful notch at a time.
The islands stop drifting as the governor finds its rhythm.
You keep your promise beneath the engine's steady golden light.

# Something that can fly
You discover a freight gondola hanging beneath the repaired island.
Its envelope is sound, but its propeller shaft has snapped.
Tavi studies the spare governor parts with a growing smile.
You remember the siege engine rebuilt for useful work.
She sees the same possibility waiting inside this abandoned craft.
You help carry an axle down the service ladder.
Pip collects dropped bolts as if they were precious jewels.
Cinder provides gentle heat while Tavi joins the metal sleeve.
You turn the propeller by hand and feel it catch.
The gondola lifts a finger's width against its mooring ropes.
Tavi paints Dawnrunner across a freshly scrubbed panel.
You climb aboard a ship built from repairs rather than conquest.

# The first flight
You release the forward rope while Tavi opens the engine valve.
Dawnrunner rises above the island with an uncertain sideways lurch.
Pip disappears beneath a coil of rope until the deck steadies.
You adjust the ballast as the wind catches the envelope.
Cinder flies ahead to show a path through the clouds.
A dark patrol balloon rises from behind the farthest isle.
You turn toward the repaired bridge instead of open sky.
The patrol cannot follow through its narrow anchor chains.
Tavi eases the engine as you slip between two silver towers.
The balloon's searchlight passes harmlessly over the empty clouds.
You emerge above a coast dotted with silent harbor lights.
Dawnrunner's first voyage has brought you where the road could not.

# A new horizon
You lower a rope ladder to the coast's tallest watchtower.
Nobody calls up, though a crowd gathers below.
A harbor keeper raises both hands in a practiced signal.
You answer with the lamp code learned during the storm.
Tavi brings the ship gently alongside the tower.
Pip finally emerges and insists on inspecting solid ground.
Cinder's song sounds startlingly loud above the silent streets.
You understand that the harbor has not chosen its silence.
You descend the ladder with your signal lamp held high.
