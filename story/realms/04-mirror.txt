# The reflected caravan
You enter the Mirror Desert behind Venn's swaying caravan.
Each dune reflects a different sky beneath the afternoon sun.
Pip growls at a reflection walking several steps behind him.
You cover it with your cloak until it disappears.
Venn laughs too quickly and urges everyone onward.
Tavi notices that his map has no marks from folding.
You ask where he learned the road, and he changes the subject.
A sandstorm approaches from the direction he calls safest.
You lead the camels into the shelter of a fallen arch.
Tavi anchors the ropes while you raise your shield against the grit.
Venn slips away during the storm's loudest gust.
You find his footprints leading toward a distant signal mirror.

# The message in sunlight
You crawl along the arch's shadow with Pip beside you.
Across the sand, Venn tilts a mirror toward a black tower.
Three flashes answer from the tower's narrow window.
You remember the shipping code stamped on the Archives order.
His signal matches its mark for valuable prisoners.
Pip nudges a second mirror hidden beneath a cloth.
You turn its face downward before it can betray your position.
Tavi arrives carrying a small lens from her toolkit.
Through it, you read the words etched into Venn's signal plate.
He has promised the Regent a keeper carrying recovered memories.
You return to the caravan without disturbing his footprints.
This time, you choose what information the guide will receive.

# A road you choose
You tell Venn that your lamp was lost during the storm.
Tavi quietly wraps an empty flask in your spare cloak.
You leave the false lamp beside the largest camel.
Venn offers to search for it and lifts the bundle instead.
You let him walk ahead while Pip tracks his scent.
The trail ends at a canyon lined with standing mirrors.
A masked patrol waits beyond the narrowest passage.
You stop the caravan before it enters their circle.
Tavi redirects sunlight onto the patrol's watching lenses.
You guide the camels through a shaded gap between the stones.
The patrol chases reflections moving along the wrong ridge.
You reach a hidden oasis with your real lamp untouched.

# The guide's reason
You find Venn at the oasis, holding the empty flask.
He raises both hands before you draw your blade.
You ask whom the Regent threatened to buy his loyalty.
Venn shows you a bracelet belonging to his imprisoned sister.
Tavi lowers her wrench but keeps the camels behind her.
You refuse to surrender the lamp or abandon his sister.
Pip noses a folded prisoner list from Venn's pocket.
You find her name beneath the mark of the nearby tower.
Venn gives you its service entrance and admits the guards change at dusk.
You let him stay, but ask Tavi to keep his signal plate.
Trust will need deeds rather than another convenient promise.
Together, you prepare a rescue the Regent did not order.

# The house of doubles
You enter the tower through a door reflected in the canyon wall.
Inside, every corridor ends with somebody wearing your face.
You lift the lamp instead of answering their whispered invitations.
Only one doorway remembers the marks of real footsteps.
Pip follows its scent while Tavi scratches arrows behind you.
A false Tavi offers a ladder leading toward the ceiling.
You notice that her toolkit hangs on the wrong shoulder.
Your shield breaks the illusion when you turn it toward her.
Behind the glass, a frightened prisoner points toward the cells.
You free him and send him back along Tavi's arrows.
The next door bears Venn's sister's bracelet on a hook.
You carry it inside so she will know who sent you.

# The compass without north
You unlock the cell and find Venn's sister repairing a broken compass.
Her name is Mira, and the instrument refuses to point north.
She says it points toward a promise its bearer intends to keep.
You promise to bring every prisoner safely outside.
The needle swings toward a hatch beneath the cell's sleeping bench.
Tavi fits the instrument into the case her father gave her.
You lift the hatch while Mira gathers the other captives.
An alarm sounds when the last cell door opens.
Venn appears above you and jams the alarm bell with his mirror.
You hold the hatch as everyone descends into the service tunnel.
The compass keeps its direction even when the tunnel bends.
You follow a promise instead of a guide's invented map.

# The broken bargain
You emerge beneath the signal tower as the patrol returns.
Venn stays behind to pull the ladder out of their reach.
A guard orders him to honor his bargain with the Regent.
You hear Venn answer that the bargain is over.
He drops the signal plate into the empty canyon below.
Tavi unfolds a rope bridge between two ruined balconies.
You catch Venn's wrist as he jumps the widening gap.
Mira helps pull him onto the safe side.
The patrol's mirrors fall dark without their guiding signal.
You lead the prisoners toward the oasis under ordinary stars.
Venn takes the rear position instead of offering directions.
For the first time, his silence feels honest.

# Beyond the glass
Mira gives you the promise compass as payment for the rescue.
You ask her to help travelers recognize false signals.
Venn chooses to remain and rebuild the oasis shelter.
You return his empty flask but keep the lesson it taught.
Tavi marks the honest road on a map that now has folds.
Pip sleeps beside Mira until the camels are ready.
You leave the desert with fewer illusions and a clearer destination.
Ahead, two forest banners face one another across a divided valley.
You follow the compass toward a promise nobody there agrees upon.
