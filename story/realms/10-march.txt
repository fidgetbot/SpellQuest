# The gathered roads
You land Dawnrunner beside a camp spread across three meeting roads.
Oak wardens share watchfires with willow healers beneath joined banners.
The courier from Briarwood brings messages between their tents.
You recognize the potter from Dawnrest serving soup in his rescued bowl.
Tavi finds her father repairing wagons beside the walking barricade.
Neri has sent signal crews to the highest hills.
You unfold the star route where everyone can see it.
The Regent's army stands between the camp and the Last Library.
You refuse a charge that would leave the refugees undefended.
Instead, you propose opening one safe corridor across the plain.
Each ally offers a skill you once helped them recover.
You begin with a plan that belongs to the whole gathering.

# The first crossing
You ride ahead with the oak scout to examine a ruined causeway.
Dark water fills the ditches on either side.
You recognize the same poison once poured into Rootbound's river.
The willow healers bring reed filters grown from that first repair.
You lower them into the current while the scouts keep watch.
Tavi positions the walking barricade across the weakest section.
Its folded legs become supports for a temporary bridge.
You guide the first supply wagon over the repaired stones.
Pip trots beside it as if personally inspecting every wheel.
The wagon reaches the far bank without losing a crate.
You signal the camp to send the healers next.
Behind you, the corridor begins with food and medicine rather than weapons.

# The enemy's eyes
You climb a low hill to locate the Regent's signal towers.
Their mirrors turn whenever a wagon leaves the camp.
Venn arrives carrying the plate you once took from him.
He asks for a chance to make his knowledge useful.
You give him a task with a partner instead of sending him alone.
Mira watches the nearest mirror while he reads its flashing orders.
Together, they identify the signal for an empty road.
You ask Neri's crew to copy it from a distant ridge.
The enemy's searchlights swing toward the false report.
You lead another line of wagons through their abandoned view.
Venn stays at his post until the last cart passes.
Trust returns in the shape of work done when nobody applauds.

# A shield for many
You hear the first warning horn from the western ditch.
A column of bound guardians advances toward the refugee road.
You see black collars beneath armor made in Clockwork City.
Tavi recognizes the quick-release joints from the old assembly line.
You hold the front guardian's hammer against your shield.
An oak scout climbs its back and opens the collar housing.
You cut the command thread when Tavi gives the signal.
The freed guardian turns to block the column behind it.
One by one, your allies repeat the release instead of smashing bodies.
The advance slows as protectors remember whom they should protect.
You lower your aching arm when the last collar falls.
The road now has defenders taller than its watchtowers.

# The bridge at dusk
You find the final river crossing burning beneath an artificial red sunset.
The Regent's troops have soaked the bridge ropes in ink.
Cinder circles above the flames but cannot extinguish them alone.
You recall the mountain vent that carried dangerous heat away.
Tavi turns Dawnrunner's propeller toward the burning span.
The strong current of air bends the flames away from the ropes.
Cinder gathers the loose fire into a harmless rising plume.
You cross with wet blankets and cover the remaining knots.
The potter's rescued bowl passes water along the waiting line.
Soon, dozens of ordinary hands finish what magic only began.
You test the bridge before allowing the families to cross.
Dusk settles blue and quiet over a road the Regent meant to destroy.

# The missing wagon
You count the arrivals and discover one supply wagon missing.
Its track turns toward an abandoned quarry beyond the bridge.
Pip finds a scrap of the courier's familiar leather beside the turn.
You take a small rescue party while the main column keeps moving.
The wagon lies tipped between two stones at the quarry entrance.
The courier shelters beneath it with three frightened passengers.
A command balloon hangs above them with its anchor wedged below.
You climb the anchor rope while Tavi steadies the wagon.
Inside the basket, you remove the unattended signal receiver.
The balloon rises freely and lifts the fallen wagon clear.
Your allies guide the passengers out before the wheels settle.
You return to the corridor with every name accounted for.

# The road remains
You reach the edge of the March as dawn lights the repaired crossings.
Behind you, watchposts pass signals along the open corridor.
The Regent's remaining troops retreat toward the capital's walls.
You do not follow them into a fight the refugees no longer need.
Instead, you help build shelters where the three roads divide.
The freed guardians carry beams beside the people they nearly attacked.
Venn and Mira mark honest routes for travelers arriving late.
Neri's crews teach the lamp code to children from Dawnrest.
You see skills spreading beyond the companions who first learned them.
Tavi says the kingdom is becoming harder to silence.
You leave supplies enough for the road to stay open without you.
Your next journey can begin because the last victory will endure.

# The narrow company
You gather Pip, Tavi, and Cinder beside Dawnrunner's mooring rope.
The others will protect the corridor while you seek the Crown.
Ilyan's star route points through a valley absent from ordinary maps.
You lay the promise compass beside it and watch both directions agree.
The courier hands you letters for people still trapped in the capital.
You pack them beside the teacher's note rather than beneath your armor.
The camp raises no battle cry as you leave.
Instead, a hundred small lights guide your ship toward the valley.
You carry their quiet confidence into the shadow of the Last Library.
