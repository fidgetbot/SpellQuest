# The flooded steps
You descend stone steps that disappear into green water.
Pip tests a floating book and wisely chooses your shoulder instead.
You tie a rope between the shore and a leaning lamp.
A boatkeeper named Sella rows out from the submerged entrance.
She reads the courier's seal and lets you aboard.
You help her push past shelves drifting like broken fences.
Beneath the surface, blue windows flicker with trapped memories.
Sella explains that the lake rose when the histories were erased.
You catch a loose stair rail before it strikes the boat.
Together, you steer into the upper reading room.
A painted waterline creeps higher while you unload your pack.
You promise to find the missing floodgate instructions before nightfall.

# The borrowed breath
You search a dry cabinet for the floodgate ledger.
Its drawer contains only a glass lamp and an empty hook.
Sella says the lamp can hold one rescued memory.
You lower it beneath the water and watch a blue spark enter.
A remembered staircase appears inside its curved glass.
You follow its outline along the flooded gallery.
Pip stays with Sella while you test each submerged step.
You use the shield as a brace against the current.
At the bottom, a door presses shut beneath the water's weight.
You pull its counterweight rope until the hinges yield.
An air pocket waits beyond, bright with phosphorescent shelves.
You take a breath beside books untouched by the flood.

# The keeper's record
You find a bronze cylinder strapped beneath a reading desk.
The memory lamp reveals fingerprints along its hidden seam.
You twist the seam and unroll a thin metal page.
Its first portrait shows a gentle archivist beside a broken crown.
You recognize the Regent's face without its shadowed mask.
The record says he once guarded every name in the kingdom.
You turn the page and see a fire consuming the oldest shelves.
He promised that no history would ever be lost again.
The final picture shows locked doors where readers once gathered.
You understand that his protection became a prison.
A tremor shakes dust from the ceiling before you can continue.
You secure the record inside your cloak and find another exit.

# The current's teeth
You surface beside Sella as the reading room begins to tilt.
Pip leaps into your arms with an indignant bark.
A whirlpool opens where the entrance staircase stood.
You lash two tables together to steady the boat.
Sella poles toward a maintenance hatch above the waterline.
You climb first and lower the rope for her.
Something silver flashes beneath the raft's trailing corner.
You lift your shield as a mechanical eel snaps upward.
Its teeth close on the silver border instead of the rope.
You twist aside and expose the ink clogging its gills.
One clean stroke frees it from the Regent's command.
The eel dives ahead and illuminates the maintenance tunnel.

# What the flood concealed
You follow the freed eel to three enormous water wheels.
Only one turns, dragging the others against their locks.
Sella finds the ledger sealed inside a waterproof inspection box.
You hold the lamp over diagrams faded almost beyond reading.
The stored memory restores a sequence of colored handles.
You match the first handle to a notch beside the wheel.
Water surges backward and reveals a trapped worker's platform.
You secure the platform before opening the second gate.
Sella guides the worker onto the ladder while you brace it.
The final handle refuses to move beneath a hardened ink shell.
You warm the shell with your blade until it crumbles.
All three wheels turn together and the lake begins to fall.

# The voices below
You return to the lower stacks as their floors emerge.
Hundreds of small bottles roll against the newly exposed shelves.
Inside each bottle, somebody's memory taps for release.
You recognize a child's drawing in one wavering reflection.
Sella finds labels listing families from towns across the kingdom.
You cannot carry every bottle, so you seek their shared lock.
The labels all point toward a silver cabinet at the center.
You open it with the bronze page's concealed key.
Every bottle uncorks at once without breaking its glass.
Streams of blue light hurry through the windows toward home.
The lamp keeps only the staircase memory you still need.
You watch Sella remember her mother's name and begin to laugh.

# A record worth saving
You discover the Regent's seal stamped on a recent shipping order.
Its cargo was sent to Clockwork City beneath military escort.
The order calls the stolen memories replacement instructions for workers.
You copy the route onto a page Sella dries by the stove.
An armored skiff appears outside before the ink is dry.
You close the shutters as its hook catches the balcony.
Sella raises the floodgate enough to loosen the skiff's anchor.
You cut the boarding rope while Pip scatters empty bottles.
The startled soldiers tumble safely into their own waiting boat.
A returning wave carries them beyond the library's outer wall.
You reopen the shutters to a lake shining in ordinary sunlight.
The Archives will no longer send prisoners disguised as cargo.

# The road of gears
You place the bronze record where every visitor can read it.
Sella gives you the memory lamp with its first blue spark.
You leave the shipping order with a warning for arriving travelers.
The rescued worker volunteers to keep the floodgates turning.
Pip finds the dry staircase and proudly leads you upward.
From the shore, you hear readers arguing happily over restored books.
You carry the copied route toward smoke beyond the eastern hills.
Somewhere ahead, a city is building weapons from stolen memories.
You tighten your shield strap and follow the sound of gears.
