# The white walls
You reach the Unwritten Citadel beneath walls blank as untouched paper.
No banners mark its towers, and no windows reflect your ship.
Tavi lands Dawnrunner behind a ridge beyond the listening gates.
You hide the unfinished Crown beneath a plain travel cloth.
Pip finds a narrow path left by supply carts.
Cinder scouts above it without singing into the waiting silence.
You discover names scratched into the stones beside the service entrance.
Workers have been remembering themselves where the Regent would not look.
You add no command, only a mark showing the safe road behind you.
Tavi opens the service latch with a tool made in her freed city.
You step inside carrying every place that helped you arrive.
The blank walls cannot tell you who you are.

# Different doors
You find three inner gates joined to one central lock.
Each gate demands something the others refuse to accept.
Tavi studies the machinery while Cinder examines a cold furnace.
Pip follows a scent beneath a door too narrow for your armor.
You realize the gates were built to defeat a traveler arriving alone.
Tavi takes the gear passage with the repaired governor key.
Cinder warms the furnace without giving it his whole flame.
Pip slips beneath the narrow door carrying your lightest signal cord.
You wait at the central lock rather than choosing someone to abandon.
Three small signals arrive in the order you agreed upon.
You turn the handle when all your companions are ready.
The gates open together on a room full of stolen names.

# The waiting letters
You recognize addresses from the courier's bundle inside the name room.
Some belong to prisoners working in the citadel's lower kitchens.
You deliver the first letter through a serving hatch.
Its recipient begins crying before she finishes the familiar handwriting.
You show her the safe-road mark scratched outside the gate.
She passes it along without waiting for a magical command.
Tavi disables the bells above the kitchens' exit.
Cinder lights a path that stays warm without burning.
Pip escorts the first group toward Dawnrunner's hidden landing place.
You remain until the workers can guide one another.
The citadel loses its servants without a battle.
Beyond the empty kitchens, you hear the Regent calling for an answer.

# A room without yesterday
You enter a chamber where the walls display victories you never won.
The Regent offers a version of your journey without mistakes or fear.
You see Pip rescued before he ever needed help.
Tavi stands beside a city that never suffered and never changed.
You notice that nobody in the pictures chooses what happens next.
The Crown grows cold beneath its travel cloth.
You tell the Regent that a perfect picture is not a living kingdom.
He erases the pictures and reveals a frail man beneath the dark mantle.
You recognize the archivist from the burned library record.
His anger rises when you speak his teacher's name.
You keep the blade lowered and hold the note where he can see it.
The chamber shakes because the words are not under his control.

# The final command
You brace the shield as the Regent orders the floor to disappear.
The promise compass points toward a real foundation beneath the illusion.
You step where its needle leads while the false abyss opens around you.
Tavi joins you after guiding the last workers outside.
Pip follows the scent of your footprints through the shifting dark.
Cinder lights each solid stone as you reach it.
The Regent commands your companions to forget your name.
You answer with memories nobody was forced to surrender.
Tavi recalls the city bridge built from a walking barricade.
You remember Pip choosing your pack at Briarwood's repaired gate.
Cinder sings the three notes first offered beside his cage.
Their freely given memories hold against the Regent's command.

# The last empty spaces
You uncover the Crown and show the Regent its remaining open sockets.
He offers to fill them with stolen words and end your struggle.
You refuse a victory built from the same theft you came to stop.
Tavi guards the doorway while you continue the work honestly.
Pip stays close when the mantle's shadows test your shield.
You set another earned jewel where an empty space waited.
A name returns to the room's wall without anyone ordering it there.
The Regent reaches toward it, surprised by a memory he had buried.
You place the teacher's note beside the growing line of names.
He reads its first words while the shadows hesitate.
You keep learning instead of turning his hesitation into a threat.
The Crown's light becomes steadier as its final spaces narrow.

# The keeper lets go
You turn the teacher's note so the Regent can read its ending.
The sentence asks him to protect lives without trapping them.
He remembers the handwriting before he remembers how to answer.
You offer the released ledger page as proof that his locks can open.
Outside, the rescued workers call to one another across the court.
Their ordinary voices reach the chamber through its unguarded doors.
The Regent hears a kingdom continuing without his instructions.
He loosens one hand from the mantle's clasp.
The shadows cling harder, feeding on the fear he has carried.
You raise the shield between him and the thing his grief became.
Tavi opens the shutters while Cinder brings daylight into the room.
Together, you make space for a choice that must be his.

# A kingdom unwritten
The Regent releases the clasp and lets the shadow mantle fall.
You hold the Crown steady as the loose darkness gathers for one last strike.
Pip pulls the teacher's note clear of its reaching edge.
Cinder lights your shield while Tavi keeps the shutters wide.
You set the next jewel beneath a sky no longer hidden by the citadel.
Across the distant roads, restored beacons answer the Crown's growing light.
The former Regent steps behind your shield and speaks his own name freely.
You lift the almost-complete Crown toward the darkness, ready to return every stolen voice.
With your final earned jewel in place, you release the Crown's light and watch the last shadow dissolve into a morning the whole kingdom can choose.
