# The Shattered Lexicon — narrative continuity

## Canon and pacing
The player is a keeper with an initially unlit blade. Pip, a small fox, is rescued in Briarwood; he is not already a companion at the opening. The Ink Regent is a former archivist whose fear of losing people and histories became coercive control. Understanding his grief never excuses his actions. The resolution restores agency rather than deleting him.

## Consequences and payoffs
Freed guardians protect roads and later the coalition. Archives machinery informs Clockwork repair. Tavi's repurposing philosophy produces a skyship. The desert betrayal teaches verification rather than permanent distrust. Rootbound supplies protect refugees. Skychain signals defeat coastal silence. Coast evidence leads to the Observatory. Observatory knowledge changes the final confrontation. The coalition's open roads let the Library share its originals. The final choice answers the teacher's note.

No realm resets these achievements. No recurring scout rescue, firebird capture, or beacon loop. Scene structures vary: investigations, evacuation, repair, stealth, negotiation, travel, defense and confrontation.
